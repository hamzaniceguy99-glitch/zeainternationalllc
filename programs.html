Program 01 · Find them, then check them

Supplier sourcing

Most sourcing problems are avoidable at the start. We identify candidate suppliers for your product, put the same specification in front of each one so quotes can actually be compared, check company details and references, and manage the sample stage before any production commitment is made.

Import & export coordination

An order that nobody is chasing slips. We keep the timeline visible from purchase order to arrival, make sure terms of sale and delivery are agreed in writing rather than assumed, and coordinate the handover points where shipments most often stall.

  • Importers and exporters

Freight & shipping

Freight quotes are hard to compare because they include different things. We gather quotes on the same basis, explain what each one actually covers, book with licensed carriers and forwarders, and keep you informed while the shipment is moving rather than after it's late.

Commercial documentation is where shipments get held up. We prepare and check the commercial invoice, packing list and supporting certificates for consistency, and get the file to your customs broker complete. The filings themselves are made by licensed professionals, not by us.
